MPEG-DASH through the HLS player

This sample loads a static MPD and bridges its fragmented MP4 segments to the existing Omakase HLS playback engine.

Initializing player...

The default manifest uses two video renditions from Envivio's public DASH test stream, without audio so it also works in browsers without AAC support. Loading does not autoplay; press Play after it is ready.

For audio/video playback in a browser with AAC support, load https://dash.akamaized.net/envivio/EnvivioDash3/manifest.mpd.

Use a static, single-period MPD with fragmented MP4 and SegmentTemplate addressing. Live, DRM, and embedded subtitles are not supported yet. Streams must allow CORS.
